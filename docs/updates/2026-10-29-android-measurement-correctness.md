# Android pipe inventory and ventilation measurements — 29 October 2026

RohrCalc now retains geometric pipe water volume at zero declared flow. A 20 mm internal diameter over 10 m contains 3.141592654 l whether idle or flowing. Idle velocity, Reynolds number, friction, loss and head remain zero. Existing dimension/number guards and positive-flow hydraulic calculations remain intact; the stored geometry and archive format are unchanged.

LüftungsCalc now lets a terminal exist before it has been measured. Leave the measured-flow field blank for unmeasured; enter numeric zero for an actual zero reading. Incomplete supply/extract totals and deviations remain unavailable instead of summing missing readings as zeros. The screen and DE/EN reports identify unmeasured terminals; a side without terminals is labelled separately. Saved adjustment visits retain their original measured/unmeasured state after subsequent edits.

The ventilation filename remains `vent-commissioning-v1.json`. New documents use internal schema 2 and explicit JSON null for unmeasured values. The reader accepts schema 1 numeric values unchanged, including measured zero, and represents absent/null optional readings as unmeasured. Migration is in memory on load; only a subsequent explicit successful save writes the new schema. Old app versions reject schema 2 under their existing protected-load behavior. Current readers still reject unsupported future schemas, malformed numbers, unexpected fields, noncanonical damage, trailing content and stale revisions; writes remain atomic. No app/package identifiers, purchases or existing saved measurements are reset.
