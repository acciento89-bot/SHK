Privacy Notice for SHK Werkstatt

SHK Werkstatt and the earlier SHK calculator apps work without user accounts, advertising, tracking or a Kamilunavo backend for calculation and service data.

1. Controller

Piotr Kaminski – Kamilunavo, Otto-Braun-Straße 14, 40595 Düsseldorf, Germany. Contact: contact@kamilunavo.com.

2. Scope

This notice covers SHK Werkstatt on iOS and iPadOS, and the earlier KälteCalc, LüftungsCalc, HeizkörperCalc, RohrCalc and AnlagenCheck apps. The general website Privacy Policy also applies when visiting kamilunavo.com.

3. No registration

The SHK apps do not require an account or sign-in. Kamilunavo does not receive customer or project data from the app. You may enter customer names and site addresses for your own project documentation on your device; these data are not sent to Kamilunavo.

4. Calculation and service data

Customer and site details, heating and refrigeration measurements, pipe and ventilation data, water inventories, radiator values, system parameters, check limits, checklists and calculated results are processed on the device for the core functions and are not transmitted to Kamilunavo servers.

5. Local data

Where an app stores projects, favorites, preferences, notes or recently used values, this storage remains local to the device. There is no Kamilunavo cloud storage or server-side synchronization. Projects can be deleted in the app. Device backups depend on your iOS/iPadOS settings and Apple services.

6. Sharing and exporting

When you use a share or report function, the app passes the selected content to the operating system's share interface. You then choose which app or person receives it. Kamilunavo does not receive the shared content.

7. No tracking or advertising

The SHK apps do not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking. App, measurement and calculation data are not sold for advertising purposes or shared with advertising networks.

8. Purchase and Pro access

SHK Werkstatt provides free basic features and an optional permanent Pro purchase through Apple In-App Purchase. The app verifies purchase status using Apple StoreKit to enable additional projects and PDF export and restore purchases. Apple handles payment and billing; Kamilunavo does not receive complete payment-card details in the app. Earlier standalone SHK apps may continue to be offered as paid downloads.

9. Support

If you voluntarily contact Kamilunavo by email or through the support page, the information you provide is processed only to handle your request and in accordance with the general Privacy Policy.

10. Data-subject rights

Subject to statutory requirements, rights may include access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Requests can be sent to contact@kamilunavo.com.

11. Changes

This notice is updated if product functions, services used or legal requirements change. The version published on this page is the applicable version.
